How booking works

Four steps from search to keys

  1. Search by area & time

    Enter where and when. The catalog only shows cars that are free in that window.

  2. Book or request

    “Instant book” cars are confirmed right away. Others are approved by the owner, usually within an hour.

  3. Pick up with the checklist

    Take a few photos, note fuel and kilometres in the app, get the keys.

  4. Return & review

    Park where you picked it up, repeat the checklist, rate the owner. Done.

Hour or day

Pay for the time you actually drive

Every car has an hourly and a daily price. When the hours add up to more than a day, you automatically pay the daily rate.

By the hour

For errands, a trip to IKEA, a meeting across town. Minimum 2 hours, then per started hour.

Example: 4 hours in a compact ≈ 260 kr

By the day

For weekends away, moving house or visiting family. 24-hour blocks, 200 km per day included.

Example: a weekend in an SUV ≈ 1 580 kr

FAQ

Questions from renters

What if I return the car late?

Extend the trip in the app before it ends — if the car is still free, it's charged at the normal rate. Late returns without an extension cost 150 kr per started 30 minutes.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Every trip includes Basic protection with third-party liability and roadside assistance. You can lower your deductible with Standard or Premium protection when booking. See protection plans.

How does the deposit work?

We hold 1 500 kr on your card when the trip starts and release it within 3 days after the return, once the checklist shows no new damage.

Can someone else drive?

Yes, if they have their own verified Rently profile and you add them to the trip before pickup.

What happens if the owner cancels?

You get a full refund straight away, and we help you find a similar car nearby.
