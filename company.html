About

Started in Copenhagen, built for neighbours

Rently began in 2023 when two flatmates noticed one of their cars barely moved on weekdays, while the other kept paying for taxis. Today owners all over Denmark share their idle hours with verified drivers nearby.

Neighbours first

Trips happen between real people a few streets apart. We design for trust on both sides.

Open about money

Every fee is shown before booking. No hidden extras at the end of a trip.

Fewer cars, more use

Every shared car means fewer new ones on the road and less space for parking.
