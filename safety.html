For owners

Your car is covered while it's rented

During an active trip our insurance takes over, so your own policy and no-claims bonus aren't touched.

  • Up to 1 000 000 kr damage cover per trip
  • Loss of income paid while the car is being repaired
  • Photo checklist at pickup and return as evidence
  • Your deductible depends on your plan — see owner pricing

If something happens

  1. Make sure everyone is safe. Call 112 in an emergency.

  2. Call our 24/7 line — we arrange roadside help or a tow.

  3. Take photos of the damage in the app.

  4. We handle the claim with both sides within 5 working days.

FAQ

Safety questions

Who is responsible if something happens during a trip?

The renter is responsible up to the deductible of their protection plan; our insurance covers the rest. Owners are covered by our policy during active trips, not their own.

How do you verify renters?

We check a government ID and driving licence, match them with a selfie and look at the licence history. Renters under 25 can only book standard cars.

What if the car isn't as described?

Don't start the trip. Report it in the app within 30 minutes of pickup and we'll refund you or help you find another car.
