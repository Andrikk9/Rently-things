FAQ

Questions from owners

Who is responsible if something happens during a trip?

Our insurance covers collision, theft and vandalism during active trips. Your own insurance is not affected. Renters accept the damage rules before booking, and our support team handles claims within 5 working days.

Can I decline a booking?

Yes, you can decline any request before pickup. Keep your calendar up to date to avoid last-minute cancellations — a high decline rate makes your car show up lower in the catalog.

What counts as “idle” availability?

Any time your car isn't in personal use: evenings after work, weekends, holidays. You set the schedule and can change it every week. The minimum trip is 2 hours.

How do I set my rental price?

Our price tool suggests hourly and daily rates based on the model, age, mileage and local demand. You can go above or below the suggestion and add discounts for longer trips.

How do I get paid and when?

Payouts go to your bank account 2 days after each trip closes. The platform fee (20–30% depending on your protection plan) and any agreed damage costs are deducted first. See pricing for owners.
