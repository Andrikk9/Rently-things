Terms of use

The rules for owners and renters on Rently.

Contents

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

Rently Inc. runs a marketplace where car owners rent out their cars to verified drivers for short periods. Rently is not a party to the rental itself, but handles payments, insurance and support.

2. Your account

You must be at least 21, give correct information and keep your login safe. One person, one account.

3. Listing a car

Owners must have the right to rent out the car, keep it roadworthy and describe it honestly, including any known damage.

4. Booking and payment

The price shown at the time of the request is binding. We charge the renter when the owner approves and pay the owner 2 days after the trip.

5. Damage and protection

Damage is handled under the protection plan chosen at booking. The pickup and return checklist is the main evidence.

6. Cancellations

Free up to 24 hours before the trip. Later cancellations are charged 50% of the trip price.

7. Liability

Rently is liable only for its own services and to the extent required by Danish law.

Privacy policy

What data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Contents

  3. Who we share it with

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Data we collect

Name, contact details, ID and licence data, payment details, trip history, messages and the photos from checklists.

2. Why we use it

To verify people, run bookings and payments, handle insurance claims, prevent fraud and improve the service.

3. Who we share it with

The other side of your trip (only what is needed), our insurer, payment provider and ID-check partner. We never sell personal data.

4. How long we keep it

As long as your account is active, and up to 5 years afterwards where bookkeeping law requires it.

5. Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data, and complain to Datatilsynet. Write to privacy@rently.example.
