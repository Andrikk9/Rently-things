FAQ

Accordion or linked anchors to help page—wireframe shows copy slots only.

Who is responsible if something happens during a trip?

Answer: Our insurance policy covers damage from collision, theft, or vandalism during active rentals. You set a deductible amount upfront. Renters pre-approve damages per our damage guidelines. Major incidents trigger our support team to investigate and process claims within 5 business days.

Can I decline a booking?

Yes, you can decline any rental request during the approval window before pickup. We recommend blocking your calendar in advance to prevent last-minute cancellations. High decline rates may reduce your visibility in search results, so planning availability upfront works best.

What counts as "idle" availability?

Answer: Any time your car isn't in personal use—evenings after work, weekends, vacation days. You set your own schedule and can change it weekly. Minimum availability is typically 4 hours for weekend trips or full days for longer rentals. You control exactly when your car is available.

How do I set my rental price?

Answer: Our pricing tool suggests rates based on your car's make, model, age, mileage, and local demand. You can adjust above or below the suggestion. View competitor pricing in your area and adjust seasonally. Rates apply per day; multi-day bookings can qualify for discounts you set.

How do I get paid and when?

Answer: Payments deposit directly to your linked bank account 2 days after each trip closes. We deduct our platform fee (typically 20–30%) and any damage claims. You can view earnings history and payouts anytime in your dashboard. Minimum payout is $50; smaller amounts roll to your next trip.
