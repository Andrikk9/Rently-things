FAQ

Pricing questions

Can owners change the price after I book?

No. The price you see when you send the request is the price you pay. Owners can only change prices for future bookings.

Are there discounts for longer trips?

Owners can add weekly discounts (usually 10–20%). They're applied automatically and shown in the price breakdown.

When do I pay?

Your card is charged when the owner approves the trip. Extras added during the trip are charged when it closes.

Is VAT included?

Yes, all prices on Rently include Danish VAT.
